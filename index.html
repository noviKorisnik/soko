A modern puzzle experience by Dejan Katasic, noviKorisnik, and Boneco Biruta. Push boxes, plan ahead, and master handcrafted challenges.

How to Play

Goal: Push all boxes (■) onto target dots (●).

Movement: Use Swipe or Arrows/WASD to move your player (●).

  • You can only push one box at a time.
  • You cannot pull boxes.
  • Undo any move if you get stuck!
